Manleluag Spring Protected Landscape

Manleluag Spring Protected Landscape in Mangatarem, Pangasinan is a protected natural area open to the public, best known for its natural hot spring pools. Visitors can soak in the mineral-rich waters while surrounded by the surrounding forest landscape, making it a relaxing stop for those looking to unwind away from typical tourist spots.

Location Mangatarem, Pangasinan
Best Time to Visit Year-round
Activity Hot spring bathing, nature walks
Entrance Fee Minimal entrance fee (check locally)
