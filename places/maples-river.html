Maples River

Maples River is a hidden gem in Pogonsili, Aguilar, Pangasinan, known for its calm, clear waters framed by lush mountain scenery. It's a favorite cooling-off spot during the summer months, offering a peaceful escape from the heat with plenty of natural shade and quiet, scenic surroundings that make it feel far removed from the usual crowded tourist spots.

Location Pogonsili, Aguilar, Pangasinan
Best Time to Visit Regular Season (It's dry in summer and too dangerous in rainy season due to intense water flow)
Activity Swimming, sightseeing
Entrance Fee Free
